## 1. Bayesian Uncertainty Propagation for Agentic RAG Pipelines: A Proof-of-Concept Study on Multi-Hop Question Answering

- **Authors:** Louis Donaldson, Connor Walker, Koorosh Aslansefat, Yiannis Papadopoulos
- **Published:** 2026-07-01
- **Categories:** cs.AI
- **arXiv:** [2607.00972](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00972v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.00972v1)
- **Matched topics:** ai_agents, large_language_models, ai_automation, generative_ai

### TL;DR
This paper presents a proof-of-concept study on Bayesian uncertainty propagation for Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, specifically for multi-hop question answering. The approach uses a Bayesian Network to estimate system-level uncertainty and identify potential failure points across the workflow. The study evaluates the effectiveness of this approach on two datasets, StrategyQA and HotpotQA, using GPT-3.5-Turbo and GPT-4.1-Nano.

### Why it matters for these roles
* **Data Analyst**: This work highlights the importance of uncertainty estimation in RAG pipelines, which can inform data-driven decision-making in applications like Offshore Wind (OSW) maintenance decision support.
* **Data Scientist**: The study demonstrates the effectiveness of Bayesian uncertainty propagation in identifying potential failure points in RAG pipelines, which can be applied to various multi-hop question answering tasks.
* **AI Engineer**: This work showcases the use of Bayesian Networks to estimate system-level uncertainty, which can be applied to other AI systems that involve multi-stage reasoning pipelines.
* **LLM Engineer**: The study evaluates the performance of LLMs (GPT-3.5-Turbo and GPT-4.1-Nano) in RAG pipelines, providing insights into the strengths and limitations of these models.
* **Generative AI Engineer**: This work highlights the importance of uncertainty estimation in generative AI systems, which can inform the development of more robust and reliable models.

### Key technical takeaways
* Bayesian uncertainty propagation is used to estimate system-level uncertainty in RAG pipelines.
* A Bayesian Network is employed to propagate uncertainty signals from individual stages to the system level.
* The approach uses semantic divergence and generator self-evaluation to derive uncertainty signals.
* The study evaluates the effectiveness of this approach on two datasets, StrategyQA and HotpotQA, using GPT-3.5-Turbo and GPT-4.1-Nano.

### Method & implementation
The approach involves the following steps:
1. **Uncertainty estimation**: Each stage in the RAG pipeline (planner, evaluator, and generator) produces uncertainty signals using semantic divergence and generator self-evaluation.
2. **Bayesian Network**: The uncertainty signals are propagated through a Bayesian Network to estimate system-level uncertainty.
3. **Evaluation metrics**: The approach is evaluated using Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve (AUROC), Area Under the Accuracy-Rejection Curve (AUARC), Expected Calibration Error (ECE), and Brier Score.

## 3. Can Agents Generalize to the Open World? Unveiling the Fragility of Static Training in Tool Use

- **Authors:** Song-Lin Lv, Weiming Wu, Rui Zhu, Zi-Jian Cheng, Lan-Zhe Guo
- **Published:** 2026-07-01
- **Categories:** cs.AI
- **arXiv:** [2607.01084](http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01084v1) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.01084v1)
- **Matched topics:** ai_agents, large_language_models, generative_ai

### TL;DR
The paper "Can Agents Generalize to the Open World? Unveiling the Fragility of Static Training in Tool Use" by Song-Lin Lv et al. investigates the limitations of Large Language Model (LLM) agents in real-world scenarios due to distributional shifts. They propose a controlled sandbox environment and Perturbation-Augmented Fine-Tuning to enhance agent robustness.

### Why it matters for these roles
* **Data Analyst**: Understanding the limitations of LLM agents can inform data collection strategies and data quality control measures to ensure accurate and robust models.
* **Data Scientist**: This work highlights the importance of considering distributional shifts in real-world scenarios, which is crucial for developing accurate predictive models.
* **AI Engineer**: The paper's findings on the fragility of static training in tool use can inform the development of more robust AI systems that can adapt to changing environments.
* **LLM Engineer**: The proposed Perturbation-Augmented Fine-Tuning strategy can be used to enhance the robustness of LLM agents, making them more suitable for real-world applications.
* **Generative AI Engineer**: This work can inform the development of more robust generative models that can adapt to changing environments and user queries.

### Key technical takeaways
* **Distributional shifts**: The paper highlights the importance of considering distributional shifts in real-world scenarios, which can affect the performance of LLM agents.
* **Perturbation-Augmented Fine-Tuning**: This strategy involves introducing disturbances to the training process to enhance the robustness of LLM agents.
* **Controlled sandbox environment**: The paper proposes a controlled sandbox environment to systematically diagnose the impact of distributional shifts on LLM agents.

### Method & implementation
The authors construct a controlled sandbox environment with a four-tier hierarchy: Perception, Interaction, Reasoning, and Internalization. They conduct a comprehensive series of experiments to investigate the impact of distributional shifts on LLM agents trained via Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning. The authors propose Perturbation-Augmented Fine-Tuning as a disturbance-based intervention strategy for SFT to enhance agent robustness.
